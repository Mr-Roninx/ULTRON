# ULTRON Synthetic Payment Universe v1.5 Specification
**Codename: ULTRON-SWU-1.5 (Economic Adversarial Reality & Causal Attribution Engine)**

## 1. System Overview
ULTRON-SWU-1.5 subjects the autonomous revenue recovery agent to **adversarial economic reality**. It introduces natural recovery competition, adverse intervention consequences (fatigue, opt-out, churn), shared gateway externalities, multi-tier economic accounting, and shadow evaluation across 11 competing policies.

---

## 2. Invariant Rules
1. **Three-Domain Separation**: `WORLD` (reality), `AGENT` (observable slice at $t \le T$), `EVALUATOR` (latent oracle, future outcomes, counterfactuals).
2. **Deterministic Financial Authority**: The LLM remains semantic intelligence advisor; all money movements occur via `AdversarialDoubleEntryLedger`.
3. **Temporal Causality**: Blind observation firewall strictly rejects future timestamps ($timestamp > t$).
4. **Common Random Numbers**: Synchronized stochastic streams across all 11 competing policies.
5. **No Double Counting & Zero-Sum Conservation**: Incremental recovery cannot exceed actual world revenue or outstanding exposure.
