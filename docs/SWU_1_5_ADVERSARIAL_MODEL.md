# ULTRON-SWU-1.5 Adversarial Economic Model

## 1. Adverse Intervention Dynamics
Interventions are not presumed to be positive:
- **Over-Contacting Penalty**: Outreach adds contact fatigue ($\Delta \text{WhatsApp} = +0.16$, $\Delta \text{Voice} = +0.35$).
- **Opt-Out & Churn Thresholds**: Fatigue $>0.85$ triggers opt-out; fatigue $>0.92$ triggers churn.
- **Unnecessary Outreach Damage**: Interventions on payments that would recover naturally damage trust ($\Delta = -0.08$) and waste operational spend.
