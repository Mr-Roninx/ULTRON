# ULTRON Synthetic Payment Universe v1.4 Specification
**Codename: ULTRON-SWU-1.4 (Emergent Population Economy)**

## 1. System Overview
ULTRON-SWU-1.4 advances the synthetic financial world into an **Emergent Population Economy**. Rather than generating payment events directly from hardcoded scripts, payment demand emerges dynamically from the interacting behaviors of customer cohorts, merchant lifecycles, gateway load feedback, and macro shocks across multi-day horizons up to 365 days.

---

## 2. Invariant Rules
1. **Three-Domain Separation**: `WORLD` (complete reality), `AGENT` (observable slice at $t \le T$), `EVALUATOR` (latent oracle, future outcomes, counterfactuals).
2. **Deterministic Financial Authority**: The LLM remains semantic intelligence advisor; all money movements occur via `PopulationDoubleEntryLedger`.
3. **Temporal Causality**: Strict lookahead firewall ($timestamp \le t$) with zero future information leakage.
4. **Common Random Numbers**: Synchronized stochastic streams across counterfactual forks.
5. **Causal Attribution**: Provenance chain for every recovered rupee; natural recovery is explicitly isolated from ULTRON incremental lift.
