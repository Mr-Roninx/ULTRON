# ULTRON-SWU-1.5 Long-Term Customer LTV Effects

## 1. Multi-Horizon Lifetime Value
Tracks projected repeat GMV across 30, 90, and 365 simulated days:
$$\text{Projected LTV} = \text{Monthly Frequency} \times \text{Horizon Months} \times \text{Relationship Trust} \times \text{Spending Capacity}$$

A recovery action that claims ₹5,000 today but causes customer churn results in an immediate **-₹400,000+ LTV destruction penalty**.
