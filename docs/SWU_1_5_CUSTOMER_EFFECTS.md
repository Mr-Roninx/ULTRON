# ULTRON-SWU-1.5 Customer Effects

## 1. Multi-Horizon Fatigue Dynamics
- **Instantaneous Fatigue**: Channel outreach shock.
- **Rolling 24-Hour & 7-Day Fatigue**: Cumulative outreach burden.
- **Thresholds**: NORMAL ($<0.40$), ELEVATED ($0.40-0.65$), HIGH ($0.65-0.85$), CRITICAL ($>0.85$).
- **Decay Rate**: Exponential decay ($\text{factor} = 0.50^{\text{days}}$ for 24h, $0.85^{\text{days}}$ for 7d).
