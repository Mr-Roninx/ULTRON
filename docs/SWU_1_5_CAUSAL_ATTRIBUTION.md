# ULTRON-SWU-1.5 Causal Attribution

## 1. Multi-Tier Economic Attribution
- `DIRECT_INCREMENTAL_REVENUE`: Funds recovered that would have failed permanently without intervention.
- `PROBABLE_INCREMENTAL_REVENUE`: Modeled recovery with residual latent uncertainty.
- `DOWNSTREAM_INCREMENTAL_REVENUE`: Repeat GMV generated by preserved customer trust and retention.
- `NON_INCREMENTAL_RECOVERY`: Payments that self-healed naturally through issuer retry or salary inflow (credit = ₹0).
- `NEGATIVE_EXTERNALITY`: Gateway congestion fees, support ticket overhead, and customer acquisition replacement costs.
