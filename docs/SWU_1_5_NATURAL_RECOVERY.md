# ULTRON-SWU-1.5 Natural Recovery Model

## 1. Natural Recovery Paths
- **ISO 91 (Issuer Timeout)**: High natural recovery rate (65%) within 30m–4h as issuer databases recover.
- **ISO 51 (Insufficient Funds)**: Moderate natural recovery rate (28%) aligned with salary cycles.
- **ISO 14 (Invalid Card)**: Low natural recovery rate (5%).

If a payment would recover naturally in the counterfactual branch, ULTRON receives **₹0 incremental credit**.
